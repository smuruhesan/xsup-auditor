# Disclaimer & Internal Use Notice

## Internal Use

XSUP Retrospective Auditor is intended for authorized internal Support workflows.

It may process confidential customer and internal Support information.

Use it only in accordance with your organization's:

- security policies
- privacy requirements
- data-classification rules
- acceptable-use policies
- software-governance requirements

---

## Decision-Support Tool

The auditor is a decision-support tool.

It is not an autonomous authority for:

- Jira field changes
- Salesforce updates
- customer communications
- knowledge publication
- employee performance evaluation

A qualified human reviewer remains responsible for the final decision and any resulting action.

---

## AI-Generated Output

TACO and Case Chat outputs can be incomplete, inaccurate, or overconfident.

Always verify important conclusions against original evidence.

When evidence is insufficient, prefer:

```text
UNDETERMINED
```

over assumption.

---

## Knowledge Drafts

Generated KCS, Admin/Tech Guide, Runbook, Known-Issue, or Release-Note content is a draft.

Do not publish it without appropriate technical and documentation review.

---

## Security / Compliance

The design includes security-conscious controls, but the repository does not by itself represent formal InfoSec, privacy, legal, compliance, or production approval.

Do not claim formal certification or approval unless it has been explicitly granted by the relevant internal authority.

---

## No Security Bypass

The auditor must not be used to bypass:

- authentication
- authorization
- browser security
- Content Security Policy
- endpoint controls
- DLP
- download restrictions
- corporate software controls

---

## Sensitive Data

Do not commit real customer/case evidence to this GitHub repository.

Do not place credentials, tokens, cookies, passwords, or private keys in the source.

---

## Use With Caution

Stop and manually verify the case when:

- source evidence conflicts
- product applicability is unclear
- the mapped case looks wrong
- a proposed field change is unusual
- a generated artifact contains unsupported operational detail
- the result appears inconsistent with original Engineering evidence

---

## Availability / Support

The tool depends on TACopilot interfaces and browser behavior that may change.

Availability and behavior are not guaranteed unless a formal support/ownership model is established.

---

## Final Responsibility

Use of the auditor does not remove the reviewer's responsibility to exercise technical judgment and follow approved Support processes.
